Privacy Policy

MedBilling Fee Desk · Effective date: October 6, 2026

This policy applies to the MedBilling Fee Desk app for iPhone, iPad and Android, and to the web version at tp8p7c4vwr-del.github.io/delara-medbilling. MedBilling Fee Desk is made by Jose F de Lara in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, and is part of JFdeLara's Studio.

Summary

MedBilling Fee Desk does not collect any personal information or patient information.

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • Your settings, favourites and recent codes stay on your device.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any information, because we do not have any.

What stays on your device

  • The fee schedules, codes and rules are stored inside the app. Searching runs entirely on your device, works offline, and what you type is never sent anywhere.
  • Your chosen province or territory, fee skill or specialty, favourites and recently viewed codes are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. We cannot see them.
  • On Android they are excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. On iPhone and iPad they may be part of your own device backup, which only you control.
  • They are deleted when you clear them in the app (Recent → Clear, or remove a ★ favourite) or when you uninstall the app.
  • The app never asks for patient names, personal health numbers (PHN), dates of birth, chart numbers or claim numbers. Please do not enter them anywhere in the app.

Things that happen only when you choose them

  • Links to official sources and reference sites (for example government fee schedule PDFs, PubMed or the WHO ICD browsers) open in your device's browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
  • Ask SI/AI opens an outside AI website that you pick. The prompt contains only the billing code, its description, the fee skill or specialty and the schedule date, never patient details and never what you typed in the search box. You use that site under your own account and its privacy policy applies.
  • Share and Export favourites use your device's share sheet. The shared text or file contains only codes, descriptions, dates and fees. You decide where it goes. Import reads a file you pick and keeps it on the device.
  • Suggest a change in the iPhone, iPad and Android apps opens your own email app with a message addressed to delaramedbilling@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you write and send that email yourself.

Web version

  • The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use those logs.
  • On the web version, the Suggest a change form sends your message to our email through the Web3Forms form service, and only when you press Send. Your email address is optional. The form refuses text that looks like it contains patient identifiers.
  • The web version saves its offline copy and your favourites in your browser's storage on your device.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive only what you choose to send (your email address and your message). We use it only to reply and to fix or improve the app, we do not add you to any mailing list, and we do not share it. We delete it when it is no longer needed, or sooner if you ask.

Permissions

The app uses no permissions other than internet access, which is needed only to open the links above. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, calendar or health data.

Children

MedBilling Fee Desk is a professional reference for physicians and billing staff. It is not directed at children.

Your privacy rights in Canada

We follow the principles of Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act. Because the app does not collect personal information, we have none to give you access to, correct or delete. If you have emailed us, you can ask what we have and ask us to delete it. If you have a privacy concern, contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version at this address with a new effective date.
